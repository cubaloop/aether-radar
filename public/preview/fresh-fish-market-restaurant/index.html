The Ajman Market Tradition

How The Live Seafood Experience Works for You

We bridge the authentic heritage of Ajman's fishermen with a spotless, air-conditioned family restaurant. You choose the exact fish you want, determine your preferred cooking method, and watch our masters work over glowing embers.

Step 1: Inspect & Hand-Pick

Choose Hamour, Sherry, Kingfish, Seabass, or Tiger Prawns fresh from our iced display.

Step 2: Choose Your Culinary Style

Charcoal grill (Meshwi), Singary, crisp golden fry, or oven-baked in rich clay tajine.

Step 3: Feast With Traditional Sides

Accompanied by caramelized onion sayadieh rice, fresh tandoor bread, tahina, and garlic dips.
